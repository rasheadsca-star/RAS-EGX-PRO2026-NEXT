# V16 Loss Anatomy — Frozen Diagnostic Contract

Status: research-only diagnostic. This audit does not modify V16 ranking, recommendation levels, portfolio weights, or execution permissions.

## Objective

Diagnose where the accepted V16.9 blocked walk-forward selections fail, using features that were observable at each signal date. The goal is to identify stable failure families before proposing any further challenger. Outcome information is used only to label and summarize failures, never to construct signal-date features or choose cutoffs.

## Source evidence

- Primary selections/outcomes: exact V16.9 blocked walk-forward audit generated by `v16-v169-target-hit-audit.py`.
- Feature source: pinned `data/history` OHLCV, sliced strictly to `date <= signalDate` with an exact signal-date bar required.
- Minimum feature history: 60 sessions.
- Missing feature rows remain missing/neutral and are not converted into a bearish observation.

## Frozen pre-outcome feature families

For every V16 selection with sufficient history, compute only from data available through the signal date:

### Entry heat
- RSI14.
- EMA20 extension measured in ATR14.
- Distance from the 20-session high.

### Trend / momentum
- 5-session close return.
- 20-session close return.
- EMA20 versus EMA50 percentage spread.

### Volatility
- ATR14 as a percentage of close.

### Liquidity
- Median adjusted turnover (`adjusted close × volume`) over the last 20 sessions.
- Signal-day volume divided by the prior-20-session median volume when available.

### Recommendation geometry
- Entry-zone width in ATR14.
- Stop distance in ATR14 from signal close.
- Target distance in ATR14 from signal close.
- Structural reward/risk measured from the entry-zone midpoint.

### Market breadth
Computed cross-sectionally on the exact signal date from feature-ready symbols only:
- Percentage above EMA20.
- Percentage above EMA50.
- Percentage with positive 20-session return.
- Median 20-session return.

Market breadth requires at least 60 feature-ready symbols.

## Outcome and execution anatomy

Outcome labels are descriptive only:
- `NO_ENTRY`: V16 selection was not executable under the recorded open rule.
- `STOP`: executable and stop touched; same-bar target+stop remains `STOP_FIRST`.
- `TARGET`: executable and conservative target hit.
- `OTHER`: executable without conservative target or stop touch in the recorded target-hit audit.

The following are explicitly **ex-post execution anatomy**, never candidate-generation inputs:
- Next-session opening gap versus signal close.
- Whether the next session opened inside the entry zone, above it, or below it.
- Next-session high/low excursion versus the recorded stop/target.

No future/session-outcome field may be mixed into the pre-outcome feature matrix.

## Frozen descriptive binning

For each numeric pre-outcome feature:
1. Collect all finite feature values without looking at outcomes.
2. Split at the 33.33% and 66.67% empirical quantiles into `LOW`, `MID`, `HIGH` tertiles.
3. Summarize each tertile using executable count, conservative target rate, stop rate, target-minus-stop edge, positive next-close rate, and average next-close return.
4. Repeat the same fixed full-sample tertile boundaries inside three chronological folds. Boundaries are not re-fit to outcomes or to individual folds.

This is descriptive research; tertile boundaries are not trading thresholds.

## Frozen stability definition

A feature can be marked `STABLE_DIAGNOSTIC_PATTERN` only when all conditions hold:
- At least 90 feature-ready rows overall.
- At least 60 executable rows overall.
- Both extreme tertiles contain at least 20 executable rows.
- Extreme-tertile separation is material by at least one criterion:
  - absolute target-minus-stop edge difference >= 10 percentage points, or
  - absolute average next-close return difference >= 1.0 percentage point.
- The same extreme-tertile directional ordering appears in at least 2 of 3 chronological folds with at least 8 executable observations in each compared extreme tertile in that fold.

Direction is evaluated from a composite descriptive quality measure: first target-minus-stop edge; average next-close return breaks near-ties. The audit reports raw effect sizes and does not claim causality.

## Family-level guardrails

Features are grouped into families. A family may be diagnostically stable even if no new rule is allowed.

Already tested/rejected families are locked:
- `ENTRY_HEAT`: the prior outcome-blind entry-heat abstention rule failed its preregistered robustness gate. Anatomy may describe heat effects but may not resurrect this family by choosing a new cutoff on the same outcomes.
- `MARKET_BREADTH`: Breadth Regime Exposure V1 failed its frozen internal gate. Anatomy may describe breadth effects but may not retune breadth thresholds on the same outcomes.
- `RAW_MOMENTUM`: the independent raw momentum V1 expert failed its preregistered retrospective gate. Anatomy may describe momentum/trend effects but may not retune that V1 policy on these outcomes.
- `RAW_PULLBACK`: raw pullback V1 failed its frozen research gate. Anatomy may describe pullback-like effects but may not retune that V1 policy on these outcomes.

## Post-destructive-review validity correction: derived-feature degeneracy

This validity guard was added after the destructive review of the first anatomy output. It is **not a performance threshold and is not permitted to alter outcomes, bins, feature values, or effect sizes**. Its sole purpose is to reject a purportedly new family when the apparent variables are algebraic transforms of the same frozen construction and therefore do not represent independent decision degrees of freedom.

The first anatomy output exposed this issue in `GEOMETRY`: the V16 source constructs entry width, stop distance and target distance from the same ATR using fixed multipliers (`0.16`, `0.90`, `1.20`). Dividing those frozen levels by an independently recomputed anatomy ATR can make all three normalized values move together because of ATR implementation/rounding differences. Such movement is a measurement identity artifact, not evidence that wider/narrower recommendation geometry has incremental alpha.

The geometry family is therefore ineligible for a new-candidate recommendation when all of the following identity checks hold:
- stop-distance / entry-width is within 5% of `0.90 / 0.16 = 5.625` at both q33 and q67;
- target-distance / entry-width is within 5% of `1.20 / 0.16 = 7.5` at both q33 and q67;
- structural-RR q67 minus q33 is at most `0.01`, showing the underlying reward/risk construction is effectively constant.

When triggered, the original descriptive effect remains visible and is labeled `STABLE_BUT_FORMULA_DERIVED_ARTIFACT`, but `candidateEligibility=false`. This correction cannot make a losing rule look better; it can only remove a non-independent false-positive family from candidate generation.

## New-candidate rule

At most one new research family may be recommended after this audit, and only if:
- it contains at least one `STABLE_DIAGNOSTIC_PATTERN`,
- it is not one of the locked/rejected families above,
- it is not excluded by a validity/identity guard such as formula-derived degeneracy,
- its strongest feature is directionally stable in >=2/3 folds,
- the proposed next experiment can be specified without selecting an outcome-optimized cutoff.

If no unlocked independent family satisfies these conditions, disposition must be:
`NO_NEW_RETROSPECTIVE_RULE_MOVE_TO_FRESH_FORWARD_EVIDENCE`.

Even if one unlocked family qualifies, this audit does **not** grant alpha weight or production authority. The next experiment must be separately preregistered before its first outcome run.

## Data-integrity gate

The audit must fail closed on:
- a feature slice whose last row is after the signal date,
- missing or malformed signal dates,
- non-finite recommendation levels used for geometry,
- future rows entering any feature calculation.

Ordinary missing historical coverage is reported and excluded; it is not a Critical/High defect by itself.
